qbo-export — Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

qbo-export connects only to Six Feat Media's own QuickBooks Online company, with the owner's permission. It requests only the Accounting scope and uses it read-only.

Data is downloaded only to the owner's own encrypted computer, as a backup.

No data is sent to, sold to or shared with any third party. No analytics or tracking is used.

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer. Access can be revoked at any time in QuickBooks under Settings → Apps.
